Making a branded real estate reel in about two minutes

A generic video app is built for anything. A listing reel has a narrower job: show the property well and get posted the same day.

The workflow is three steps. Pick a template for the moment you are marketing, such as just listed, open house, price drop, sold, luxury or home tour. Add your photos. Export a vertical 9:16 reel and share it.

A brand kit saves the repeated typing. Set your logo, colour, phone number and website once, and they appear on every reel you export.

PropVid's real estate reel maker offers 668 reel templates, organised by those moments, and works on the web or on your phone.
