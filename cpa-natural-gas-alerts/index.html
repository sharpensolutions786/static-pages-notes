Natural gas alerts: one of the most volatile markets there is

Natural gas is one of the most volatile liquid markets, so thresholds from other commodities do not carry over. Over the last year, the front-month contract moved 2.13% on a typical day. One day in ten moved more than 6.23%, and the largest single day was 47.48%.

A 5% alert fired about 1.4 times a month, which is a sensible place to start. Anything under 3% would have been background noise.

Energy futures trade almost around the clock from Sunday evening to Friday afternoon US time, and are closed at weekends. Gaps that open over a weekend can only trigger an alert when trading reopens.
