PPF or ELSS for the ₹1.5 lakh 80C limit: the numbers side by side

Both PPF and ELSS reduce taxable income under Section 80C in the old regime. They differ on almost everything else. PPF pays a rate the government sets each quarter, currently 7.1%, entirely tax-free, and locks the money for fifteen years. ELSS is an equity fund with a three-year lock-in, no guaranteed return, and gains taxed above the yearly exemption.

At ₹12,500 a month, which is the ₹1.5 lakh yearly limit, 15 years looks like this:

OptionValue after 15 years on ₹22,50,000 put in
ELSS at an assumed 12%₹63,07,200
ELSS at 8%₹43,54,314
PPF at 7.1%, tax-free₹40,68,209

At 8%, the equity case barely beats PPF, which is the risk in a nutshell. PPF is compounded yearly, so paying monthly rather than in April lowers the first year slightly.
