TDS on a property purchase: the same ₹80 lakh flat, two very different bills

When you buy a property in India, you may have to deduct tax before paying the seller. The rate depends on who the seller is.

SellerTDS ruleOn an ₹80 lakh flat
Resident1%, only if the price or stamp duty value is ₹50 lakh or more₹80,000
NRI12.5% plus 10% surcharge and 4% cess (14.30%), no ₹50 lakh threshold₹11,44,000

For the resident, the buyer pays the ₹80,000 using Form 26QB within 30 days of the end of the month of payment, and the seller receives ₹79,20,000. For an NRI seller the buyer needs a TAN and files Form 27Q, and the amount held back is far larger. Buyers who miss this can end up personally liable.
