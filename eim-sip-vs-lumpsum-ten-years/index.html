SIP or lump sum: ₹12 lakh the same way in, 10 years later

If you have ₹12 lakh, is it better to invest it at once or to put in ₹10,000 a month for 10 years? The money is the same. What differs is how long each rupee is invested.

At the same assumed 12%, the lump sum is ahead by ₹14,03,627 after 10 years. At a poor 8% it is ₹25,90,710 against ₹18,41,657. Those figures are projections, not promises, and equity funds can fall.

The monthly route gives up some upside to narrow the range of outcomes, because it buys more units when prices fall. The table starts at ten years, when the monthly plan has finished paying in its ₹12 lakh.
