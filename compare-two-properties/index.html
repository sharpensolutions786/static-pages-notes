Comparing two flats on cost per usable square foot

The cheaper flat on the listing is often not the better value. To compare two properties fairly, put them on the same basis: carpet rate, all-in cost, and cost per usable square foot.

For each property, collect:

  • the price and the carpet area
  • the work it needs
  • the monthly maintenance

Then add the duties and fees, and decide how many years you expect to own it. Dividing the all-in cost over that period by the carpet area gives a figure you can compare across properties. A flat with higher maintenance or a renovation bill can lose to a dearer one that needs nothing.
