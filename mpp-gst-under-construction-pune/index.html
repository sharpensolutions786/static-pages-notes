A ₹80 lakh under-construction flat: GST and stamp duty add 12%

The price on the brochure is rarely the price you pay. Take an ₹80 lakh under-construction flat in Pune.

ItemAmount
GST at 5%₹4,00,000
Stamp duty and registration at 7%₹5,60,000
Taxes together₹9,60,000

That is 12% on top of the quoted price. The identical flat bought after the completion certificate carries only the ₹5,60,000 of duty, because ready-to-move property attracts no GST.

A lower 1% rate applies only to an affordable unit: carpet area up to 60 sq m in the six metros or 90 sq m elsewhere, and a value of ₹45 lakh or less. Both conditions must hold.
