How big a home loan on ₹1.2 lakh a month with a car EMI running

Banks look at how much of your income can go to EMIs, called FOIR, often 50%. Here is one worked case.

StepResult
Net monthly income₹1,20,000
Existing car EMI₹10,000
Allowed EMI at 50% FOIR₹50,000
Loan at 8.5% over 20 yearsabout ₹57.6 lakh
Property budget at 80% LTVabout ₹72 lakh

On top of that you fund about ₹5 lakh of stamp duty and registration yourself, because they are not part of the loan. The RBI caps the loan at 90% of value up to ₹30 lakh, 80% up to ₹75 lakh and 75% above.

Existing EMIs cut eligibility directly. Closing a ₹8,000 personal loan EMI can release close to ₹9 lakh of eligibility.
