Breaking an FD early: a ₹5 lakh deposit shut after 14 months

Closing a fixed deposit early costs more than the penalty line suggests. The bank recalculates interest at the rate for the period you actually held, not the rate you booked, and then takes a penalty of usually 0.5% to 1%.

Here is the example from the calculator. ₹5,00,000 is booked for 3 years at 7.5% and broken after 14 months. The 14-month card rate is 6.5%, and with a 1% penalty the effective rate is 5.5%.

ChoiceYou receive
Hold to maturity at 7.5%₹5,46,300
Break after 14 months₹5,32,902
Difference₹13,398

Two ways to avoid it are a loan against the FD, often 1% to 2% above the FD rate, and a sweep-in FD that breaks only the part you need. The FD premature withdrawal calculator takes your own rate, penalty and months held.
