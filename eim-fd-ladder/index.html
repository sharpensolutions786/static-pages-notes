FD laddering: five deposits so one matures every year

FD laddering splits one lump sum across several deposits that mature in successive years. Instead of locking ₹10 lakh for five years at one rate, you put ₹2 lakh each into 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5-year deposits.

The result is a deposit maturing every year. You can spend it, or roll it into a new 5-year deposit. After year five you hold a permanent ladder with annual liquidity, and you have reinvested at whatever rates prevailed instead of betting everything on today's.

  • Use an average rate, since longer tenures usually pay slightly more.
  • Keep each rung under ₹5 lakh per bank to stay within the deposit insurance limit.
  • When rates are falling, weight the ladder toward longer tenures.

Laddering also avoids premature-withdrawal penalties, because something is always about to mature. Try your own amount and number of rungs in the FD laddering calculator.
