Atal Pension Yojana: joining at 28 for a ₹3,000 monthly pension

Atal Pension Yojana is a government pension scheme for people aged 18 to 40 with a savings bank account. You pay until 60, then receive a fixed pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 a month for life.

Joining at 28 for a ₹3,000 pension means ₹292 a month for 32 years, which is ₹1,12,128 paid in. The contribution is set by a government table that rises with the age you join, so waiting makes it dearer.

After your death the same pension continues to your spouse, and the corpus, 170 times the monthly pension, goes to your nominee. For ₹3,000 that is ₹5.1 lakh.

One rule catches people out: since 1 October 2022, anyone who is or has been an income-tax payer cannot join.
