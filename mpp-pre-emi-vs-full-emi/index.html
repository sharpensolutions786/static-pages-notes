Pre-EMI or full EMI during construction: what the cheaper option costs

On an under-construction property the lender pays the builder in stages. Until the final disbursement you can pay pre-EMI, which is interest only on what has been paid out so far, or start the full EMI straight away.

Pre-EMI is lighter each month and popular with buyers who are also paying rent. But it repays no principal. Take a ₹50 lakh loan at 8.5% over 20 years, with 3 years of construction and 60% disbursed on average:

Pre-EMIFull EMI
Monthly payment₹21,250₹43,391
Paid during construction₹7,65,000₹15,62,082
Principal cleared by possession₹0₹3,25,700

So the cheaper monthly option leaves ₹3,25,700 of principal uncleared. Replace those inputs with your own: loan amount, rate, tenure and how much of the construction period remains.
