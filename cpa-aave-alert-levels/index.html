Aave alerts: what a 5% move means for a lending token

Aave is the largest lending protocol, and its token reacts to lending rates and total value locked. Its 2021 high of $661.69 is far above where it trades now, so "back to the top" is not a practical alert for most people.

Over the last year, a typical day moved 2.41%. One day in ten moved more than 6.92%, and the largest single day was 23.68%.

At 5% the alert fires about five times a month, roughly once a week, which suits someone who wants to look often. An 8% level, about 2.2 times a month, suits someone who only wants the big days.

The question worth asking first is which price would change your mind, then setting the alert there.
