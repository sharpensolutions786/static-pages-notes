Tenant police verification: collect the papers at handover

Most Indian cities require a landlord to report who is living in their property, and many police commissionerates now accept the submission online. It is a short task that landlords routinely postpone, and the cost of postponing it falls on the landlord if anything goes wrong.

The requirement, the form and whether it is mandatory or only advised are set locally and differ between cities and states. Check your city police portal or the local station for the current procedure before you start.

What you can prepare in any city is the paperwork. Collect it at handover, not after a notice arrives, and take it from every adult occupant, not only the person who signs the agreement. Everyone who will live in the flat should be on the list.
