Quiet hours or a loud alarm? Setting up alerts you will actually notice

An alert you never see is no alert. Most price-alert problems are really notification problems.

Two settings matter. Quiet hours stop the phone buzzing at night for levels that can wait. A loud alarm option does the opposite: for the one level you cannot afford to miss, it plays through Do Not Disturb.

A workable split is to put routine levels on a normal push notification with a sound you recognise, and reserve the loud alarm for one or two levels you have decided in advance that you would act on. Telegram can run alongside push, so an alert is not lost if one channel fails.

Keep the levels rare. A threshold set inside an asset's normal daily range will fire constantly, and you will start ignoring it, loud alarm or not.
