Indian real estate in 2026: the headline numbers and who published them

Property commentary is full of unsourced numbers. This is a short list of 2026 figures as published by named sources.

  • House prices rose 3.6% year on year and 1.1% quarter on quarter in Q1 2026-27, according to the RBI House Price Index.
  • 171,471 homes were sold across the top 8 cities in the first half of 2026, up 1% year on year, according to Knight Frank India.
  • The RBI repo rate is 5.25%, and home loan rates start around 8.0 to 8.2% (September 2026).
  • Office leasing reached 48 million sq ft across 8 cities in the first half of 2026, according to Knight Frank India.

Two cautions. These are national averages, and your own locality can differ a great deal. And a price index measures what has happened, not what will.
