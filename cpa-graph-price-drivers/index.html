What moves The Graph (GRT) price, and how big the swings are

A protocol token's price tends to react to the same few things each time. Once you can name them, the swings look less random.

For The Graph, the drivers include query volume and indexer economics. Bitcoin's direction sets the floor, because most small tokens follow it. Thin order books and borrowed positions then amplify every move in either direction.

The size of those moves is large. Over the last year, GRT moved 2.18% on a typical day, with one day in ten above 6.12%. The largest single day was 30.28%. An 8% alert would have fired about 1.2 times a month, and a 5% alert about 5 times.

That is why alert levels for a token like this should sit well outside its normal range, or they will fire constantly. If a price jump has no obvious cause on the protocol side, check what Bitcoin did first.
