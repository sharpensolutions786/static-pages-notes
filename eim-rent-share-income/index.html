How much rent is too much? A rule of thumb with real numbers

A common rule is to cap rent at 25 to 30% of take-home pay. At 30% the rest of a normal budget still fits. Past 35%, something else has to give, and it is almost always savings.

Use take-home after tax and PF, not CTC. CTC includes money you never see.

The deposit changes the maths too. Ten months' deposit is still common in Bengaluru and Hyderabad, while two to three months is usual in Delhi NCR and Mumbai. On a ₹20,000 flat, ten months locks up ₹2 lakh that earns nothing during the tenancy. Over two years at even 6%, that is roughly ₹25,000 of forgone interest.
