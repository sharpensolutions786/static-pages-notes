Cardano price alerts: how often would each level have fired?

Cardano moves on upgrade milestones and staking news, and you cannot predict when the interesting moments come. That is the case for being told about them instead of watching.

It still pays to pick levels from the data. CoinPriceAlert measured 365 daily closes on Binance. On a typical day ADA moved 2.3%. One day in ten moved more than 6.27%, and the largest single day was 15.13%.
