Internet Computer (ICP): a 31.93% day and what it does to alert levels

Internet Computer hosts web apps directly on its chain, and its token moves on unlock schedules and developer adoption. Its all-time high of $700.65 in May 2021 is far above where it trades now, so old highs are a poor guide to levels today.

Over the last year, a typical day moved 2.22%. One day in ten moved more than 8.14%, and the largest single day was 31.93%.

The typical day is calm, but the tail is long: one day in ten moved more than 8.14% and one day moved nearly a third. An 8% alert, about three a month, catches those without the daily noise a 2% level would bring.

A wide gap between the typical day and the worst day is the reason to use percentage alerts, not a single price line.
