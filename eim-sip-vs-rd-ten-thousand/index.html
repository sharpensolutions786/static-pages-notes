A fixed deposit takes a lump sum, so the honest monthly comparison for a SIP is a recurring deposit. The post office five-year RD rate used here is 6.7%. The SIP is assumed at 12%, which is an assumption and not a rate anyone offers.

The 8% column shows what a poor decade can look like. Even there the SIP edges ahead, but only just over 10 years, and the SIP has no guarantee at all. For money you need on a known date, the deposit cannot fall. For money you can leave alone for ten years or more, the SIP has the higher expected outcome and a real chance of a lower one.
