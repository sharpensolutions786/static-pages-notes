EPF over a career: what ₹50,000 a month of basic pay grows into

Your employer and you each put 12% of basic pay into the Provident Fund every month, a combined 24%. On ₹50,000 of monthly basic that is ₹1.44 lakh in the first year.

Take someone who starts at 30 and retires at 58, with 7% yearly increments and an interest rate of 8.25%, the rate notified for FY 2025-26. By 58 the balance reaches roughly ₹3.19 crore. About ₹1.16 crore of it is contributions, and the rest is interest.

Two details matter. Part of the employer share, 8.33% on wages up to the ₹15,000 ceiling, goes to the pension scheme and not to the EPF balance. And the interest rate is declared every year, so the real result will not match a flat 8.25%.
