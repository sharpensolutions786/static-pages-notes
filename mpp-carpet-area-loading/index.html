A "1,000 sq ft" flat at 30% loading gives you about 769 sq ft

Builders have historically quoted super built-up area, but you live in the carpet area. The gap between them is called loading.

Take a flat advertised as 1,000 sq ft super built-up at ₹8,000 per sq ft, so ₹80,00,000. At 30% loading the carpet area is 769 sq ft, the built-up area is about 846 sq ft, and you pay for 231 sq ft you cannot use. The real rate is ₹10,400 per carpet sq ft, not ₹8,000.

The three areas differ like this. Carpet area is the net usable floor inside the walls, including internal partitions. Built-up adds wall thickness and usually the balcony, typically 10 to 15% above carpet. Super built-up adds your share of lobbies, lifts and clubhouse, and has no legal definition.

Since RERA came into force in 2016, builders must disclose and sell on carpet area. Work out the carpet rate for every flat you compare in the carpet area and loading calculator.
