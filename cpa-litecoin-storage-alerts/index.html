Litecoin: keeping it safe and setting alerts that stay rare

Buying Litecoin takes minutes. Keeping it safe through a full market cycle, without a mistake or a scam, is what decides how you come out.

The first choice is where it lives: an exchange, a software wallet or a hardware wallet. Leaving coins on an exchange is convenient but means trusting the exchange. Once the coins are secured, you can monitor the price without moving them, which is what a price alert is for.
