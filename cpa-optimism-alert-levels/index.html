Optimism (OP) alerts: the 10% level fired under twice a month

Optimism is an OP-stack layer 2 ecosystem, and its token moves on superchain adoption and unlock schedules. It is tiring to watch by hand.

Over the last year, a typical day moved 2.7%. One day in ten moved more than 8.4%, and the largest single day was 21.08%.

OP fires more often than Render or Aave at the same 8% level: about 3.3 times a month, against 2.5 and 2.2. Moving up to 10% brings it to about 1.8 times a month.

Unlock dates are public, so it can help to place an alert before one rather than after.
